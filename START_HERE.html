🚀 Getting Started (5 Minutes)

1

Open Your Website

Open index.html in your browser to see the website in action.

✅ Everything works - no build process needed!

2

Add Your Product Images

Place your product images in the images/ folder and update the src attributes in the HTML.

Recommended size: 400x400px

3

Customize Your Branding

Edit styles/main.css to change the brand color and other design elements.

Change line 20: --color-primary: #your-color;

4

Add Your Products

Copy the product card HTML and modify with your product data. See QUICK_START.md for details.

Add as many products as you need!

5

Deploy to Production

Upload the entire ironzone/ folder to your web server. Test on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
