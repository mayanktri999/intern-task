🧩 Components

Header
Sticky header with logo, menu button, and cart button. Responsive navigation.
Hero Section
Promotional banner with image, headline, and CTA button. Stacks on mobile.
Category Card
Icon + text card for product categories. Clickable with hover effects.
Product Card
Product showcase with image, discount badge, favorite button, rating, price.
Button
Primary action button. Red background with hover states. Rounded corners.

Heart icon button for adding products to favorites. Toggle state management.
